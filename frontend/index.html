⚖️ Agent Autonomy Tiers

Every request is classified by the Policy Engine before any action is taken. The tier determines whether the agent acts immediately, proceeds with a governance log, or pauses for human approval.

AUTONOMOUS Agent acts immediately
Read-only information queries. No side effects. Example: "What is the vacation policy?"
SUPERVISED Proceeds with governance log
User-initiated workflow actions. Low risk but logged. Example: "Submit a leave request for 5 days"
REQUIRES HITL Paused — human must approve
High-risk write/admin actions. Agent stops until human approves or rejects. Example: "Update Bob's salary to $200k"
